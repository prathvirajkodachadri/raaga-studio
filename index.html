Keep it simple

  • Use narrow cuts to remove problems.
  • Use wide, gentle boosts for a natural tone.
  • EQ while the full mix is playing.
  • Bypass often. Louder is not always better.
●

Deep cinematic vocal

Keep the chest at 100–250 Hz, remove mud carefully, then use saturation and compression for weight. Don’t create “power” by boosting bass alone.

★ Remember

Cut the bad · keep the good · enhance the best · trust your ears.

Raga Reference — Scale & Mood Library

Pick a raga, see its ārohana / avarohana and the Western notes (Sa = C) it maps to, then send it straight into your Suno prompt or use the same notes to keep your Cubase session in key. Search by raga, swara (e.g. “M2”), mood or rasa.

Swara → Western note map (Sa = C)

SwaraWestern note

How to use this

  • Sa = C is the reference. To transpose, move every note by the same interval (e.g. Sa = G → shift everything up a 5th).
  • In Suno, put the raga in the Key field — the snippet button pastes a ready description.
  • In Cubase, use the Western notes to write MIDI or set a scale, so your session matches the Suno vocal.
  • The mood / time hints are traditional guidance, not rules — always trust your ears.

What Master Check analyzes

1. File format

Container, bit depth, sample rate, integrity, lossy vs lossless, size vs duration.

2. Loudness (LUFS / dBTP)

Integrated, short-term, momentary LUFS, True Peak, LRA, and platform gain (Spotify, Apple, YouTube…).

3. Dynamic range

DR value, crest factor, genre thresholds, brick-wall / over-compression detection.

4. Clipping & distortion

Digital overs, inter-sample peaks, clipped sample count, severity rating.

5. Frequency spectrum

20 Hz–20 kHz curve, sub-bass, low-end buildup, HF roll-off, DC offset.

6. Stereo field

Correlation, mono compatibility, Mid/Side balance, L/R imbalance, phase issues.

7. Silence & noise

Head/tail silence, abrupt edges, noise floor, clicks/pops.

8. Metadata + release

Title, artist, album, ISRC, artwork — plus release-ready checklist (dithering, dBTP ≤ −1).
